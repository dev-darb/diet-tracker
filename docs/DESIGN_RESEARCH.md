## 1 · Cross-product finding

What lets one product carry many capabilities with one personality — six shared
mechanisms, all structural, none aesthetic:

1. **One repeated molecule.** Revolut renders every capability — transaction,
   crypto, savings, linked bank — as the same row (leading identity mark, title,
   subtitle, trailing value/action). Airbnb's amenities, MacroFactor's log lines,
   Alma's ingredients: same move. Capabilities scale because they're instances of
   one sentence structure, not new dialects.
2. **One feedback slot.** Duolingo's outcome sheet rises from the bottom in the
   same shape for success and failure (green vs soft red, same geometry); Oura's
   banners, Uber Eats' toasts likewise. Every outcome arrives in the same place
   wearing the same form — only color and copy vary. The user learns *where the
   product speaks*.
3. **Interpretation belongs to one voice.** Oura's serif headline ("Bedtime's
   approaching", "Major signs of strain") is the product speaking; data lives in
   quiet chips underneath. The personality IS that one voice; numbers are
   furniture. No surface gets its own tone.
4. **Canvas over containers.** Airbnb and Oura bound meaning with typography,
   whitespace and imagery on an open canvas; boxes are reserved for *objects* —
   things you tap, bars that persist, cards that act. Containers mean objecthood,
   never layout.
5. **Fixed accent semantics.** One or two identity colors (Duolingo green,
   Revolut blue, Airbnb rausch) plus per-domain data colors that never change
   meaning (MacroFactor's kcal/P/F/C palette). Color is vocabulary, not mood.
6. **Variable expression is a set of sliders, not personas.** Uber Eats' home is
   loud and its checkout is quiet — same grammar with density, imagery scale and
   prose depth turned up or down.

## 2 · Product perspectives (the single-voice question)

- **Oura** behaves like it believes *your body already knows — its job is to
  translate, never to judge.* Even "Major signs of strain" arrives calm,
  explains, and prescribes rest kindly. Celebration = a small "GOAL REACHED"
  label, not fireworks. Perspective persists identically across sleep, stress,
  cycle, activity.
- **MacroFactor** believes *you're an adult who wants accurate numbers, not
  opinions.* It never praises, never scolds; food is notation (emoji-scale). The
  proof that density works — and the cautionary tale foody's founder named:
  software for food data.
- **Revolut** believes *money should feel powerful and effortless.* Confidence
  through one-tap depth and a grammar that makes 30 products feel like one.
- **Duolingo** believes *you'll quit unless this feels alive* — so celebration is
  architecture (answer → lesson → streak escalation tiers) and failure is met
  in-character, softened, never shamed.
- **Airbnb** believes *the place sells itself* — editorial restraint, content
  leads, chrome recedes; one pinned decision bar carries the transaction.
- **Uber Eats** believes *hunger decides fast* — photography is the interface;
  the anti-lesson is its promotional shouting.

## 4 · Critique reconciliation

- **Validated by external evidence:** the silkscreen copy purge (Oura/MacroFactor
  run near-zero helper prose); undo-not-confirm (category standard); correction
  as first-class (Cal AI "Fix Issue"); density instincts (MacroFactor); usuals
  first (Uber Eats reorder rails); missing outcome rewards (Duolingo tiers);
  Pantry = inventory-first (every inventory reference).
- **Still valid, solve differently:** the critique treated interpretive prose as
  a copy problem to delete. Oura shows interpretation *is* the product when it
  speaks in one warm voice at the interpretation layer. The insight card's
  ChatGPT-ish paragraphs need the voice contract (§6), not deletion.
- **Possibly overcorrecting:** stripping ALL prose everywhere — foody currently
  has almost no spoken voice left. The fix was right for control-narration,
  wrong as a ceiling for interpretation. "Insight card on one surface" is fine
  but the *reason* (Home = pure readout) should not preclude Home carrying ONE
  spoken line (the Oura pattern).
- **Contradicted by stronger evidence:** food-as-notation. The scanner reduces
  the user's photo to a 40px thumbnail; the category makes it the hero. The
  console metaphor has made food subordinate — the founder's suspicion is
  confirmed by every food reference.
- **Needs experimentation:** dark-warm vs light canvas for photography (Oura
  proves dark-warm works for calm health; food photography reads best on light;
  test both on the scan result card); DSEG7's future (signature vs costume) —
  keep on the one Home readout, measure whether it survives the warming.

## 5 · foody's single product perspective

**foody believes** the next food decision is the unit of progress — not the day
score, not the streak, the next decision — and that it can make that decision
easier because it knows your kitchen.
**foody notices** what you have, what you've eaten, what's about to expire, what
time it is, and what you're aiming at — and speaks only when noticing changes
the decision.
**foody prioritises** the action over the archive: log it, eat it, use it,
cook it. History and analytics exist to sharpen the next decision.
**foody sounds** like a food-smart friend who's economical with words: warm,
direct, specific, never clinical, never preachy, lightly pleased when something
is genuinely good.
**foody celebrates** completed real-world behaviour — a day fully logged, an
ingredient rescued from expiry, a target quietly hit — in proportion to its
magnitude.
**foody avoids** narrating its interface, reciting its philosophy, moralising
about food, or announcing its own intelligence ("AI-powered", "personalised for
you").
**foody never** shames an eating choice, fakes a number, buries an unknown, or
adopts a different personality for a different feature.

## 6 · The voice contract

One voice; parameters vary — brevity, explanation, confidence, urgency,
enthusiasm, encouragement, recommendation strength. The spoken voice renders in
sentence case (the measured voice stays mono/uppercase data). Same speaker
throughout:

- **Identification** (brevity hi / explain none): "Chicken tikka masala — ~620 kcal · 38g protein."
- **Factual answer** (brevity hi / confidence hi): "About 31g per 100g, cooked."
- **Nutrition interpretation** (explain med / judgement zero): "You're light on protein today. Another 30–40g at dinner puts you where you want to be."
- **High-calorie meal logged** (judgement zero / practicality hi): "Big one — about 1,400 kcal. Plenty of protein in it, though. A lighter evening balances the day."
- **Missed target** (encouragement low-key): "Protein came in under for the third day. Worth a look at breakfast — it's the easiest slot to fix."
- **A good day** (enthusiasm genuine, brief): "Every target hit, all three meals logged. That's the whole loop working."
- **Expiring ingredient** (urgency med / recommendation hi): "The mushrooms have about a day left. Use them before the broccoli."
- **Recipe recommendation** (food enthusiasm hi): "Use the prawns tonight. Chilli-garlic pasta with the spinach and tomatoes — about 25 minutes, and it clears three things at once."
- **Uncertain identification** (confidence explicit): "Looks like a beef ragu — best guess. Worth checking before it counts."

The tell that it's one product: always concrete, always kitchen-grounded, always
one decision ahead, never a paragraph where a sentence works.

## 7 · foody design grammar (rejection-capable principles)

1. **The Next-Decision Rule.** Every screen answers "what would I do next?"
   before it answers "what happened?". A screen that only reports is unfinished.
2. **One Row Molecule.** Anything listable — pantry item, log entry, capture,
   suggestion — renders as: leading identity (photo or glyph) · name ·
   measured value(s) · one action. A list that invents a new anatomy is wrong.
3. **Two Voices, One Speaker.** Fragment Mono owns every measured value
   (tabular, uppercase). The spoken voice owns every sentence — sentence case,
   warm, parameterized by the voice contract. A sentence set in silkscreen caps,
   or a number set in the spoken voice, is a defect. All AI text is spoken voice.
4. **Food Is the Hero Image.** The user's capture is the hero of its result;
   ingredients carry imagery at row scale; suggestions lead with the dish.
   Food may only be a glyph when no image can exist.
5. **The Silkscreen Test.** Permanent copy is etched: if it wouldn't be printed
   on the faceplate, it isn't permanent. Interpretation is spoken, at the
   interpretation layer only. Nothing ever narrates the UI.
6. **Interpretation Over Inventory.** Where a decision exists, the number
   carries a one-line read (and attribution — name the foods that caused it).
   Where no decision exists, the number stands alone.
7. **One Feedback Slot.** All outcomes land in the stamp slot: same position,
   same geometry; tone varies (green win / neutral save / amber blocked).
   Escalation tiers: value-settle (input) → stamp (completion) → day-close
   (magnitude). Never a second feedback grammar.
8. **Containers Are Objects.** A bounded plate means "this acts" — a capture
   card, a suggestion, a pinned decision bar, a toast. Groups and sections are
   bounded by type, hairline and space on the canvas. A box around a paragraph
   is a defect.
9. **Fixed Meanings, Fewer Lamps.** Orange = the primary action, green =
   confirmed/win, amber = needs-you. Data-vis gets a nutrient identity ramp.
   Cyan/red survive only as info/error semantics, never decoration.
10. **Time Is a Signal.** Expiry, meal-time, recency are data values on rows and
    inputs to suggestions ("what's for dinner" knows it's 6pm) — never alarms.
11. **Never Wait, Never Lose.** AI runs against durable artifacts in the
    background; every automatic write is undoable; work survives refresh.
12. **Provenance Wears a Tilde.** Estimates carry ~, unknowns render as ----,
    the basis is one tap away — and it is never a paragraph.

## 8 · Variable expression model

Sliders, not personas — per surface archetype:

| Archetype (surfaces) | Density | Imagery | Prose | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Capture** (scan, log) | low chrome | live camera/photo | none | shutter + landings |
| **Register** (pantry, eat) | high | row-scale | none | value-settle only |
| **Interpretation** (home, health, insight) | medium | none→small | one spoken line, expandable | quiet |
| **Decision moment** (chef suggestion, expiry, confirm-band) | low | hero dish | 1–2 spoken sentences | stamp on accept |
| **Celebration** (day-close, streak) | minimal | optional | one spoken line | the one authored moment |

## 9 · Visual evolution (KEEP / EVOLVE / RETIRE)

- **Typography:** KEEP the two-voice split and Fragment Mono for all data.
  EVOLVE the spoken voice: Archivo unlocked into sentence case with real
  weight range for interpretation lines; ALL-CAPS reserved for silkscreen
  labels and keycaps. RETIRE caps-lock as the default register.
- **DSEG7:** KEEP, exactly one instrument (Home kcal). It is the signature.
- **Colour:** KEEP chassis-dark world, orange action, fixed semantics. EVOLVE:
  warm the ink ramp slightly; add the nutrient data ramp (kcal/P/C/F identity
  colors) for data-vis; let photography carry color. RETIRE amber/cyan as
  texture (semantic uses only).
- **Spacing/canvas:** EVOLVE — sections become typographic headings + hairlines
  on the chassis; generous vertical rhythm replaces plate-edge-to-plate-edge.
- **Cards:** RETIRE seam-boxing of groups (per §10). KEEP plates for objects.
- **Food imagery:** INTRODUCE (hero capture in results, row thumbnails in
  pantry, dish leads on chef cards). This is the largest single change.
- **Icons:** KEEP the hairline bank; grow it as needed.
- **Data-vis:** KEEP sparkline/meter/scale instruments; EVOLVE with nutrient
  identity colors and attribution lists.
- **Controls:** KEEP key/keycap/input-well grammar and the new control strip.
- **Motion:** KEEP landed-action system; ADD the day-close tier. No page
  transitions (settled).
- **Panel printing (01/02/03, MODULE-speak):** RETIRE remaining instances.

## 12 · Three evolution directions

**A. Warmer Console (conservative).** Keep the console entire; add photo
evidence, sentence-case spoken lines, margin de-boxing. Low risk, low reward —
food stays subordinate; the founder's core doubt goes unanswered.

**B. The Kitchen Instrument (recommended).** One product, two materials, one
voice: *instruments measure; food is alive; one voice speaks.* Keep TE's
principles (instrument clarity, semantic color, density, tactile controls) for
everything measured. Retire the costume (caps-lock world, panel printing,
seam-boxed everything). Introduce food imagery as functional UI and the spoken
voice as the single personality layer. Canvas replaces plates except where a
plate is an object. The signature remains (DSEG7 readout, orange action key,
stamp rewards, silkscreen labels on instruments).

**C. Food-First Editorial (aggressive).** Light canvas, photography-led,
mono demoted to data chips. Highest appetite appeal; loses the identity, breaks
dark-kitchen night use, lands near Cal AI/Bevel genericness. Fails the
"recognisably foody" test.

## 13 · Recommendation

**Direction B — The Kitchen Instrument.** It keeps a signature no competitor
has (a real instrument grammar), fixes the real defect (food subordinated to
the metaphor), gives AI text one home (the spoken voice contract), and scales:
any future feature knows its anatomy — measured values in mono on instruments,
food as imagery, one voice speaking at the interpretation layer, one feedback
slot, undo everywhere.

**Test surfaces for the distill:** (1) **Scan result card** — photo becomes
hero, spoken identification line, correction prominent; (2) **Home** — spoken
interpretation line joins the faceplate, indicators unbox, day-close moment;
(3) **Pantry** — imagery rows + expiry signals + (next tranche) the resident
chef card. These three cover capture, interpretation and register archetypes.
